# Settings

Open Settings from the profile icon on Home.

## Profile

- Set a nickname and profile photo.
- The name is used for the greeting on Home.
- The photo is picked from the gallery and stored locally.
- The profile works without a Google login.

## Appearance

- **Theme**: light, dark, or follow the system.
- **Language**: Indonesian, English, or follow the phone language.
- The app name **AturMyDuit**, **Kantong**, and **Duit** are not translated.

## Manage data

- **Wallets**: add, edit, archive, or delete wallets.
- **Categories**: add, reorder, archive, or delete income and expense
  categories.
- **Purge deleted data**: permanently remove data that was soft-deleted.

## Data safety

- **Backup & Restore**: create a local snapshot and restore from a file.
- **Privacy & data**: a summary of how data is stored.

## About

- **User guide**: the documentation you are reading.
- **About the app**: version, developer, EULA, and support links.
- **Check for updates**: checks the latest release on the release page.
  Automatic checks run at most once every 24 hours. The APK is still downloaded
  and installed manually through the browser.
- **Support the developer**: opens Trakteer. Donations are optional and do not
  lock any feature.

## EULA

The terms are available in two languages and follow the app language: `LICENSE`
for Indonesian and `LICENSE.en` for English.
