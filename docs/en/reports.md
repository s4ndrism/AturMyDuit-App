# Reports and charts

The **Charts** tab shows your income and expense patterns.

## What the report contains

- **Total income** and **total expense** for the selected range.
- **Income trend** and **expense trend**, bucketed by day, month, or year.
- **Category composition** to see where the money goes.
- **Transfer volume**, shown separately because transfer principal is neither
  income nor expense.
- A paginated transaction list.

## Filters

Tap **Filter** to choose:

- time range: 7 days, 30 days, or custom;
- wallet;
- category;
- merchant name.

The number of active filters appears on the button. Use **Reset filter** to
return to all data.

## A note on the numbers

- Reports use the same data as the Transactions tab.
- Transfer principal is not counted as income or expense.
- The admin fee is counted as an expense.
- If a chart is empty, loosen the filters or choose a wider range.
