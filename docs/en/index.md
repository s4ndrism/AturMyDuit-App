# AturMyDuit Guide

AturMyDuit is a personal finance tracker for Android that runs entirely on your
device. No account, no server, and the core features keep working offline.

This guide explains every feature in version 1.0.0, from installation to
backing up your data.

## How the app works

- **Local-first.** Every transaction, wallet, budget, and bill lives in a
  SQLite database on your phone.
- **Balances come from the ledger.** A wallet balance is money in minus money
  out, not a number typed by hand.
- **No account.** The profile is just a local name and photo.
- **Two languages.** Indonesian and English, or follow the system language.
- **Offline.** The only network access is the update check against the public
  release page.

## Need help?

Open an [issue](https://github.com/s4ndrism/AturMyDuit-App/issues) on the public
repository. Include the app version from About, your phone model, and your
Android version. Never attach personal transactions or backup files to a public
issue.
